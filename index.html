This Guide

Every pudding starts with the same base — then you build your flavor and sweetness using two simple rules: pick any combination of flavors totaling 3 tablespoons, and any combination of sweeteners totaling 3 tablespoons.

The Universal Tofu Base  ·  Always Use This

  • Silken tofu, drained & patted dry12 oz
  • Unsweetened plant milk2 tbsp
  • Pure vanilla extract1 tsp
  • Sea salt1 pinch
  • Your Flavor Mix3 tbsp total
  • Your Sweetener Mix3 tbsp total

Yields 2 servings. Scale proportionally.

  • Drain tofu and pat dry thoroughly with paper towels.
  • Add tofu, milk, vanilla, and salt to a blender or food processor.
  • Add your chosen flavors and sweeteners. Blend 60–90 seconds until silky smooth.
  • Taste and adjust. Blend again briefly if changed.
  • Refrigerate at least 1 hour. Stir and serve cold.

Making Date Paste

  • Pit 4–5 Medjool dates and soak in warm water for 10 minutes.
  • Drain, reserving the soaking water.
  • Blend dates with 2–3 tbsp of the soaking water until completely smooth.
  • Refrigerate up to 1 week. Freeze in ice cube trays for longer storage — 1 cube ≈ 1 tbsp.

Topping Guide

Master Tips

  • Always drain and pat dry (tofu) — excess moisture is the only thing standing between you and a thick pudding.
  • Stir chia seeds twice — once at mixing, once 5 minutes later, to prevent clumping.
  • Blend longer than you think (tofu) — a full 90 seconds eliminates all graininess.
  • Taste before chilling — pudding tastes less sweet after refrigerating, so slightly over-sweeten.
  • Chill at least 1 hour (tofu) / 4 hours (chia) — texture firms up and flavor deepens.
  • Stores up to 5 days refrigerated in sealed jars. Stir before serving.
